FAQ

Solar questions, answered simply.

Still unsure what system fits your roof? Speak directly with Solar X for a site-specific assessment.

Is solar worth it in Maharashtra?+

For many properties, rooftop solar can reduce dependence on grid electricity. Suitability depends on roof area, shade, usage pattern, tariff and applicable policy.

What is net metering?+

Net metering measures electricity imported from and exported to the grid, with billing handled according to the applicable DISCOM regulations.

How often should solar panels be cleaned?+

Frequency depends on dust levels, nearby construction, birds, rainfall and roof conditions. Visual checks help determine the right schedule.

What warranty do solar panels have?+

Warranty varies by brand and component. Many modules are sold with long-term performance warranties; exact terms should be checked in the selected product documentation.

Is EMI available for solar installation?+

Financing availability depends on current lenders and eligibility. Contact Solar X for the options currently available for your project.

How much maintenance does solar need?+

Solar PV is generally low-maintenance, but cleaning, cable checks, inverter inspection and generation monitoring are recommended.

How much roof space do I need?+

Roof area depends on module dimensions, access paths, shading and system size. A site survey provides the most reliable answer.

Can solar work during cloudy weather?+

Panels still generate under diffuse sunlight, but output is usually lower than under strong direct sunlight.

Can I install solar on a metal shed roof?+

Yes, many commercial and industrial systems are installed on suitable metal roofs using structure and fastening solutions matched to the roof type.

Do you provide solar water pumps?+

Yes. Solar X lists solar water pump solutions among its services. Project sizing depends on pump load, head, water requirement and site conditions.
